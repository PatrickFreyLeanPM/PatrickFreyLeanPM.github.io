## Expertise

Dan Olsen is renowned for his expertise in product discovery, validation, and scaling product teams. He focuses on lean product development, user‑centred design, and data‑driven decision making.

## Contributions

- Authored *The Lean Product Playbook*, a widely‑cited guide on applying lean principles to product management.
- Frequent speaker at industry conferences and workshops, sharing practical frameworks for product discovery and delivery.
- Active contributor to the product management community through blogs, podcasts, and mentorship programs.

## Services

- Product management coaching and consulting for startups and established enterprises.
- Workshop facilitation on lean product processes and roadmap development.
- Strategic advisory for product teams looking to implement data‑driven discovery practices.
