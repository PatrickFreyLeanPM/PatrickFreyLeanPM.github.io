Ash Maurya is an author, entrepreneur, and creator of the Lean Canvas methodology—a framework for startup validation that has become a staple in lean startup circles. His work focuses on bridging the gap between domain expertise and startup execution through lean methodology and AI.

**Areas of Expertise:**
- Startup validation and the Lean Canvas framework
- Continuous innovation and the 100X Founder concept
- AI co-founders and automated startup guidance through LEANSpark
- Founder economy and validation cycles

**Contributions to the Product Community:**
- Created the Lean Canvas, a visual framework for startups that adapts the Business Model Canvas for early-stage validation
- Author of "Running Lean," which provides a systematic approach to building products using lean methodology
- Developed LEANSpark, an AI co-founder platform that guides founders through 90-day validation cycles using the Continuous Innovation Framework
- Shares weekly insights on startup validation, lean methodology, and the Founder Economy through his YouTube channel

**Current Work:**
Ash is building in public at leanspark.ai, where he explores the intersection of domain expertise + AI + lean validation to create what he calls the "100X Founder." His belief is that the biggest untapped founder pool consists of domain experts inside companies who are solving hard problems daily but lack the startup playbook—AI closes the execution gap while lean methodology provides the validation framework.
