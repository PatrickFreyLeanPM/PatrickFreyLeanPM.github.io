<!-- The Method Section -->
<section class="landing-section landing-section--alt">
  <div class="landing-inner">
    <h2 class="landing-section-title">Three perspectives. One clear priority.</h2>
    <div class="landing-method-grid">
      <div class="landing-method-card">
        <h3>CU — Customer Value</h3>
        <p>Identify user needs and gaps. Determine whether each backlog item creates real value for the people using your product. This is the "why" behind the work.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="landing-method-card">
        <h3>BU — Business Value</h3>
        <p>Cluster items by revenue impact, retention, and strategic goals. Keep your business interests in view alongside customer needs. Balance both to survive and grow.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="landing-method-card">
        <h3>DE — Development Effort</h3>
        <p>Estimate the cost of delivering each item in story-points. Find the highest-value, lowest-effort wins. Make the most of your team's capacity.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

<!-- Why CUBE Section -->
<section class="landing-section landing-section--alt">
  <div class="landing-inner">
    <h2 class="landing-section-title">Why CUBE — not ICE, not MoSCoW?</h2>
    
    <blockquote style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
      ICE prioritization targets idea banks and is suited for early-stage idea evaluation. CUBE targets product backlogs — where the work items are concrete and the goal is to maximize outcomes while using development capacity as efficiently as possible.
    </blockquote>

    <h3 style="font-size: 1.4rem; margin-bottom: 20px;">The Origin Story</h3>
    <p>CUBE was developed over years of real-world product management at ETAS GmbH (a 100% Bosch subsidiary), while leading the development of EHANDBOOK — a software tool for automotive engineers. EHANDBOOK won 3rd place in the automotive industry's Product of the Year 2016 award, voted by readers of <em>Elektronik</em> and <em>Elektronik automotive</em>. CUBE was not invented in a workshop. It was forged under actual prioritization pressure.</p>
